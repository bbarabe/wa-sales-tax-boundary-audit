# Washington sales-tax boundary verification

**November 20, 2026 · Q4 2026 · Washington general retail sales tax**

The evidence supports asking Washington to reconcile competing jurisdiction records and asking Intuit to explain a reproducible invoice calculation. The strongest geographic evidence places the Woodinville building in location code **1735**. The clearest file defect is at **98284-9508**, where Washington's SST release assigns two counties and two rates to the same nine digits on the same day. A separate statewide check found **184** five-digit fallback rates above a lower rate recorded in the same ZIP. ## What the test invoice established

The November 20 repeat used the existing November 5 test invoice, one $100 taxable workshop, a fixed ship-from and automatic sales tax. All **31** ship-to inputs reproduced the prior jurisdiction labels and amounts. At the same winery address, STE 101-A showed **$8.90** and STE 101 showed **$10.40**. STE 105 also showed **$8.90**, although USPS gave one result for that suite, on +9051, and the state ZIP+4 record gives 10.4%. STE 108 and STE 109 each showed $10.40. The invoice was restored and the edits discarded. The panel identifies tax agencies. The numeric location codes in the comparison were inferred from those labels and rates. [Observed agency and amount for each input](../results/2026Q4/quickbooks_recheck.csv) · [Setup and controls](quickbooks-online-tests.md)

QuickBooks and DOR AddressRates agreed on jurisdiction for **13 of 31** nonrandom inputs. Omitting the ZIP extension, retrying an unlisted unit against a blank-unit row and favoring a single-number row together reproduced **31 of 31** QuickBooks jurisdictions. Two exact-field models reproduced 19 and 14 respectively. This fit supplies a concrete request for Intuit's normalized input, data vintage, match level and chosen record. The 31 observations include repeated variants at one site. They cannot establish the vendor's implementation or estimate a statewide error rate. [Three-model replay and sample-library comparison](../results/2026Q4/test_inputs_comparison.csv)

## What the public authorities establish

Washington's sourcing hierarchy starts with receipt at the seller's business location and then addresses receipt elsewhere. DOR includes qualifying live interactive workshops in its live-presentation guidance, subject to exceptions. SSUTA distinguishes transactions at the seller's business location from the purchaser-address lookup procedure. Washington's liability-relief statute has its own conditions, including proper use for subsection (1). A ship-to preview, a successful address lookup and a determination about a prior return answer different questions. DOR should confirm the winery reporting code and any action for prior returns. [RCW 82.32.730](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.32.730) · [RCW 82.32.430](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.32.430) · [Live presentations](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/live-presentations) · [SST sections and Washington certificate items](sst-rules.md)

USPS returned **13 candidates** for STE 101-A, including the entered suite on +9051. STE 101A and the deliberately unlisted STE 9999 produced the same pattern. The no-suite search also returned several extensions. The presence of an echoed unit supplies weaker evidence than the single-result STE 105 control. The published replay discloses which candidate it used. The venue's own listing reverses the street-name order and omits the unit. [Input-by-input postal notes](../tests/test_inputs_standardized.tsv) · [USPS public form](https://tools.usps.com/zip-code-lookup.htm?byaddress) · [Venue listing](https://evergreenfamilywines.com/tasting-rooms/)

King County gives the winery building point as 47.73229011, −122.14063994. In DOR's Q4 polygon coordinate system it falls in exactly one location code, **1735**, about **61 feet** inside the boundary. To reproduce, select county address point `{061D163D-D103-47A9-B78B-BCAE9F444D02}`, request its geometry with the DOR shapefile's .prj as the output spatial reference, then select polygons containing the point and measure to the selected polygon's boundary in US survey feet. The transformed point is (1236641.89195337, 879095.59835768). It represents the building and carries no suite. AddressRates returned 1735 for the venue but commonly supplied ZIP 98052 and result code 4, which calls for validation.
