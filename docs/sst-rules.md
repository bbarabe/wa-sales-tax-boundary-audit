# Washington and SST rules used in this audit

**A physical sale, an address lookup and relief for a calculation error are three different questions.** Washington sources retail sales under RCW 82.32.730. Under its general hierarchy, receipt at the seller's business location comes first, followed by where the purchaser receives the product when known. DOR's guidance includes qualifying live, interactive workshops within the October 1, 2025 expansion, subject to its exceptions. For an in-person class the physical venue matters. The appropriate reporting code and treatment of prior returns for this taxpayer are questions for DOR. [Washington sourcing statute](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.32.730) · [DOR guidance on live presentations](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/live-presentations)

| Authority | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| SSUTA §§305.A.6–7, 306, 307.B, 309.A.2–4 and 310.A | For the purchaser-address due-diligence procedure, use the USPS lookup, CASS-certified software or another Board-approved standardizer. Try address records, then the applicable nine-digit ZIP, then the five-digit ZIP as provided. Different provisions govern transactions that do or do not require a complete street address. §307.B excepts receipt at the seller's business location from §§305.A.6–7. §309 does not turn a private ship-to simulation into a ruling on an event's physical site. [Agreement amended through May 13, 2026](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/docs/default-source/agreement/ssuta/ssuta-as-amended-through-05-13-26-draft---clean-final.pdf) |
| SST Technology Guide SST160.9, chapter 5, printed pp. 25–26, 29, 39 | The Guide expects a unique valid row at each record level for the date. An address-level query can omit the ZIP extension. The other address fields must match, with house and secondary numbers evaluated against ranges. The Guide lists secondary fields and does not specify a supplied-unit wildcard or a narrowest-range tie-break. An address range shares its street, city and ZIP extension. Two simultaneously effective ZIP+4 rows covering the same nine digits present a clearer uniqueness problem than two street ranges separated by their ZIP extensions. [Technology Guide](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/docs/default-source/technology/technology-guide-october-2022.pdf) |
| SSUTA §§305.A.6 and 308.C | In a nine-digit ZIP area with several rates, the state assigns the lowest combined rate. For a five-digit ZIP area with several rates, the Agreement permits highest, lowest or blended. These are instructions for building the database. They do not authorize selecting a cheaper street row. [Agreement, §§305 and 308](https://www.streamlinedsalestax.org/docs/default-source/agreement/ssuta/ssuta-as-amended-through-05-13-26-draft---clean-final.pdf) |
| Washington Certificate of Compliance v2026.0, published August 17 | 305080 and 308042 confirm the nine-digit minimum. 305081 and 308041 state “Lowest rate” for five digits. 305115 answers yes to using the state's address-based records for §306 relief. 305116–117 confirm the ZIP fallbacks. 306010 cites RCW 82.32.430. [Washington's filed responses](https://sst.streamlinedsalestax.org/CC/form/14955) |
| RCW 82.32.430(1), (5) | Subsection (1) covers rate-calculation errors resulting from proper use of DOR-developed and provided GIS technology by someone collecting and remitting the tax. Subsection (5) separately addresses specified registered sellers and CSPs, with a temporary hardship provision. The audit cannot establish that a particular QuickBooks calculation or a prior return qualifies. [Washington statute](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=82.32.430) |

A composite SER identifier serves reporting. This project separately maps Washington records to familiar DOR codes for comparing labels. **Postal evidence.** The USPS site can display a single entry, several address and range candidates, a five-digit ZIP with no extension, or no result. Selection of a candidate in a replay is an explicit input. The public lookup cannot reveal the result of Intuit's standardizer or prove that a tenant occupies a unit.
